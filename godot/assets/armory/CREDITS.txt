Each weapon uses its own pair of posed meshes, opening fingers, padded glove
surfaces, cuffs and sleeves. Glove and cloth textures were generated for this game.
Adaptations include new gun rigs, grip positions,
muzzle markers, texture resampling, illustrated finishes and inventory renders.
The adapted composite models and rendered derivatives are distributed under CC BY-SA 2.5.
Enemy portraits derive from the patrol model, detailed in assets/patrol/CREDITS.txt.
